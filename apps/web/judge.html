How a payment moves

Explanatory · this page starts no transfer

01

Customer

A payment is raised from web, offline queue, or a feature phone — one contract for every channel.

02

Risk gate

Scored before any money is touched. Low risk passes, medium asks for step-up, high is blocked outright.

03

Funds reserved

The payer's amount is held, not yet moved, so a failure later releases it instead of stranding it.

04

Cryptographic ledger

A balanced double-entry record is appended and hash-chained to the entry before it.

05

Settled

The hold is committed and the payee credited. The receipt can be verified independently.
